About DriveWise

DriveWise is a student-built car dealership review application. We believe dealership experiences should be easier to explore, compare, and discuss.

Our mission

Help car shoppers discover dealerships by state, read reviews and share their experiences. Data and personnel shown on this demo site are fictional examples, not real dealership endorsements.
